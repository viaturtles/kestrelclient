Try: the sidebar, the installation tiles, the green button and its arrow, tabs, settings categories.

Section sweep

Panel only, title bar and sidebar stay still. Wing-angled band crosses bottom-left → top-right (reverse when moving up the sidebar). Page swaps while covered. 640ms · enter 42% · covered 16% · exit 42%

Quick page change

Old page lifts away, new content rises in a short stagger. Used inside pages: tabs, project pages, settings. out 120ms · in 380ms, 45ms stagger

Sidebar & tabs

One highlight glides between items with a slight spring, so the eye follows where you went. 380ms · cubic-bezier(.3,1.35,.5,1)

Installation switch

Cover art cross-fades with a slow settle-in zoom; name and details slide up. art 420ms + 1.4s drift · text 360ms

Launch

Lifts 1px on hover with a light sweep across the texture, presses down on click, then shows “STARTING…” with a thin progress line. Demo only: nothing launches here.

Menus, cards, toggles

Menus grow from the control that opened them (160ms). Cards lift 2–3px and their art zooms 5%. Toggles slide with a spring. Reduce motion turns everything into instant changes.
